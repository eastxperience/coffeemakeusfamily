Checked 12 November 2026, Asia/Jakarta. Undated pages are not evidence of new industry trends.

- [National Coffee Association: What is coffee?](https://www.aboutcoffee.org/origins/what-is-coffee/) — opened through the NCA official URL; coffee beans are seeds inside coffee fruit, dried/roasted/ground for brewing. Used only for the engineering script's basic sequence; no health or numeric yield claims adopted.
- [FAO: Arabica coffee manual — harvesting and processing](https://www.fao.org/4/ae939e/ae939e08.htm) — opened; processing fruit produces green coffee ready for roasting. Historical manual, not current market evidence. Its blanket natural-process quality judgement is not adopted.
- [Blender official command-line manual](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/advanced/command_line/) — headless/background Python rendering. Actual pilot uses official Blender 4.5.9 LTS arm64 binary, verified against its official SHA-256 file. Version recorded in render provenance.
